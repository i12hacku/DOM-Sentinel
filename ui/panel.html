Single-page apps

      
Apps built with React, Vue, Angular and similar frameworks change pages without reloading. DOM Sentinel notices the new address and marks the previous results as out of date.

      
      
Reviews

      
Reviews you give findings (confirmed, false positive, accepted risk) are saved per site in this browser and reapplied on every scan.

      

      
Scan history

      
The last two scans of each page are kept in this browser so new scans can show what's new and what's fixed.

      

      
What gets checked

      
Passive scans read this page, its response headers, cookies and scripts. Active checks also request a handful of well-known paths on the same origin. Only test sites you own or are authorised to test.
